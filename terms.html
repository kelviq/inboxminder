Your license

When you buy InboxMinder you receive a license key. A license is tied to the Mac it was activated on; to move it, deactivate it in Preferences (or with inboxminder license deactivate) and activate on the new machine. If a Mac is lost or wiped before you could deactivate, write to us and we will release the key.

One license may watch several mailboxes on that Mac through profiles. It may not be shared between people or installed on Macs belonging to different users; each person needs their own key.

The app checks the license with Kelviq from time to time. If it cannot reach us, it keeps working for 14 days on the last good answer, so an outage on our side never stops your mail. Only an explicit "invalid" answer from us, or 14 days with no answer at all, closes the gate; the app then stops working as described below, and nothing you have is deleted.

Without a license

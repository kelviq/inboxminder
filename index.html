Privacy

Nothing is sent to us.

There is no InboxMinder server. No account, no sync, no analytics.

We can't read your mail.

Not a promise. We never receive it, so there is nothing for us to read.

It all runs on your Mac.

Keys, the index and every draft are made and kept on your disk. Pick a local model and nothing leaves it at all.

Why we built it

Built for our own inbox first.

We're a small team at Kelviq, and Gmail is our support desk. We built InboxMinder because mornings went to email that didn't need us, and afternoons to the same replies again.

Now every email is read, the noise stays out of the way, replies are drafted the way we'd write them, and quiet threads come back to us. Nothing has ever gone out that we didn't send ourselves.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Anything else, write to hi@kelviq.com. A person answers.

Is it a help desk?

No, and that's the point. No second inbox, no ticket numbers, no seats. Customers email you as they always have, and you answer from Gmail with the reply already written. See where each help desk feature lives.

Can it send an email on my behalf?

No, and it can't be set up to. It writes drafts, adds labels and files Linear issues. Sending is the one thing it is built never to do.

Will my customers be able to tell?

Only if you tell them. Replies are written from your own past emails, at your usual length, from your own material. Most go out with a word or two changed, or none.

Can it mind support@ and hi@ at once?

Yes. Add the second mailbox as its own profile, with its own settings, docs and voice. Both live in the same menu bar.

What does it need from Google?

Your own Google sign-in, with a click-by-click guide and a video. The permission stays with your account and can be revoked any time. Nothing of ours sits in between.

Which AI does it use?

Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or any compatible service. Or a local model with Ollama or LM Studio, so your mail never leaves the Mac. You pay the provider directly.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

A wrong label is one click to change, a wrong draft one click to delete. When unsure, it does nothing except mark the email as needing you.

Is it a subscription?

No. $29 once, updates included. The only running cost is your own AI key, paid to the provider.

Does it work with Outlook?

No. Gmail only, from a Mac, and everything it does shows up in Gmail on every device.

Polish in my tone

Write it fast. Send it polished.

Select what you typed anywhere on your Mac and choose Polish in my tone, or copy it and press ⌃⌥P. It never adds a fact, a promise or a sign-off you didn't write.

What you typed

hey adarsh, we looked in to the payment isue and it were a delay on the banks side, the money shoud reflect by monday. sorry for the trouble

→
Ten seconds later

Hey Adarsh, we looked into the payment issue and there was a delay on the bank's side. The money should reflect by Monday. Sorry for the trouble.

Anywhere you type

Gmail, Notes, Slack, a browser form. Select the text, right-click, choose Polish in my tone, and it's replaced in place. A keyboard shortcut of your choosing does the same.

Or through the clipboard

Copy, press ⌃⌥P or click the menu-bar item, paste. Works in every app, with no extra Mac permissions to grant.

Two strengths

Grammar, spelling and your tone, or grammar and spelling only when you want the wording left exactly as you put it.

A copy editor, not a writer

It never adds a fact, a number, a promise, a greeting or a sign-off, never drops a link, a name, a question or a TODO, and never changes what you committed to.
